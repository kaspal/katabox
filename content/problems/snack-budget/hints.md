To buy as many snacks as possible, which snacks should you never skip in favour of a dearer one?
---
Any set of k snacks costs at least as much as the k cheapest snacks. So buy cheapest first.
---
Sort the prices and add them up from the cheapest until the next one would go over the budget. Sorting is O(n log n); looking for the cheapest remaining snack again and again is O(n²).
