Look at the two legs of the route as vectors: AB and BC. The turn depends only on how BC is rotated relative to AB.
---
The 2-D cross product `u.x · v.y − u.y · v.x` of two vectors is positive when `v` points to the left of `u`, negative to the right, and zero when they are parallel.
---
Compute `(bx − ax)(cy − by) − (by − ay)(cx − bx)`: positive → "left", negative → "right", zero → "straight". All in integers, so there is no rounding.
