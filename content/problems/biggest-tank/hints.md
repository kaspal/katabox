Trying every pair is about 2·10¹⁰ pairs. Start with the widest tank, the first and last boards. Which of the two could possibly be part of a better tank?
---
Any narrower tank that keeps the **lower** of the two boards is no taller (the lower board still limits it) and narrower, so it is worse. That board can be dropped for good.
